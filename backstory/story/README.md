# Layn — Story Draft

This directory contains the prose version of Layn's backstory, developed separately from `backstory/notes.md`.

`backstory/notes.md` remains the source of truth for story decisions, canon constraints, unresolved details, and planning.

The story itself is organized by **Part → Section → Subsection**, with each subsection intended to represent a scene or tightly connected sequence.

## Current structure

### Part I — The Child of House Freth

- I.1 — Beneath the Spider's City
  - I.1.1 — Menzoberranzan
  - I.1.2 — The Boy
  - I.1.3 — A Normal Day

Future sections will be added as they are drafted.

## Narrative approach

The story combines two lenses:

- **Layn's perspective:** an intimate view of his life and development as a young drow.
- **Omniscient perspective:** a wider view of Menzoberranzan, drow society, politics, religion, customs, and history, including information Layn does not yet understand.

Worldbuilding should be embedded in lived scenes wherever possible rather than presented as encyclopedia-style exposition.

The prose should be its own voice. *Homeland* is a reference for immersive worldbuilding and narrative architecture, not a template for copying R. A. Salvatore's prose style.
