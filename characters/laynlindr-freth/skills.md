## Important simulation rule: Reliable Talent

Reliable Talent applies **only to ability checks** that use a skill or tool proficiency.

For those checks, a natural d20 result from 1–9 is treated as 10 before adding modifiers.

It does **not** modify attack rolls or saving throws.

## Important simulation rule: Psi-Bolstered Knack

If Layn fails an ability check using a proficient skill or tool, Layn may spend one Psionic Energy Die and add the roll. The die is spent only if the check succeeds after adding the die.
