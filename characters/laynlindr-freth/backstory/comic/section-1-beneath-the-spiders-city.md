# Page 1 — No Sky

### Panel 1 — Full-page splash

**Camera:** Extreme wide shot across the cavern.

**Image:** Menzoberranzan fills a gigantic, arrowhead-shaped subterranean cavern. Towers rise from the floor and climb immense calcite formations. Stalactites disappear into darkness above. Bridges and walkways span huge gaps, joining towers carved directly from living rock. Narbondel, the city’s great central pillar, stands in the distance with a faint band of orange light around it. Tiny magical lights reveal fragments of the city; individual drow are almost impossible to distinguish.

**Narration:**
> There was no sky above Menzoberranzan.
>
> There had never been one.

**Purpose:** Establish scale, darkness, and the city as a character in its own right. Do not show spiders walking through the city.

# Page 2 — Living Darkness

> **Variety matrix:** P1 wide / cavern architecture / raking side light — P2 extreme close-up / carved spider relief + passing hand / hard rim light — P3 detail montage / magical light sources / cold multi-source glow — P4 interior medium / candle-lit noble hall / warm low key light.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Wide architectural shot looking up through the cavern.

**Image:** Great columns climb from the depths toward an unseen ceiling. Enormous stalactites hang above them like the teeth of a sleeping beast. High on the cavern roof, distant paths and houses cling to the stone above the city.

**Narration:**
> There was only stone, rising so far above the city that even the sharpest eyes could not find where the cavern ended and darkness began.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Extreme close-up of a carved spider relief set into a doorway pillar; a drow hand passes near it, not touching.

**Image:** A spider worked into the stone of a doorway, edges worn smooth by centuries. A dark-skinned hand with slender fingers passes close by without touching — a gesture of taught caution rather than fear. Raking light from one side carves the relief out of deep shadow.

**Narration:**
> Bridges and walkways crossed the empty spaces between them, joining towers carved directly from the living rock.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Detail montage.

**Images:** Narbondel’s orange glow climbs the central pillar in the distance while blue, green, and violet faerie fire outlines towers, gates, and house compounds. Pale fungi cling to cavern walls. Vast regions remain untouched by either light source.

**Narration:**
> In places, magical lights burned with cold violet or blue radiance. Elsewhere, pale fungi clung to the cavern walls and gave off their own dim glow.
>
> The light was never enough to defeat the darkness. It only shaped it.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Interior medium shot inside a candle-lit noble hall of House Freth.

**Image:** Inside one of the great houses: a long low hall of dark stone, pointed arches, weapons displayed with purpose, spider motifs carved into pillars. A few servants move quietly along the walls; a noblewoman passes with her chin high. The scene is intimate, warm, and entirely at ease with the dark — the interior counterpart to the vast cavern of Panel 1.

**Narration:**
> To someone from the surface, Menzoberranzan might have seemed to exist in perpetual night.
>
> To the drow, it was simply home.

# Page 3 — What the City Claims

> **Variety matrix:** P1 extreme-wide high angle / city layers / cool ambient — P2 detail montage / carved spider motifs / frontal formal light — P3 close-up / drow face studying a carving / low warm side light — P4 medium-wide / porter + noblewomen at a crossing / cold key light — P5 near-black / single lit doorway / one warm light in void.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** High establishing shot of the city’s vertical layers.

**Image:** Houses occupy towers and galleries at different heights. Bridges connect territories that are visibly controlled by different powers.

**Narration:**
> The city had been built in darkness by people who had learned to live inside it so completely that they no longer thought of darkness as the absence of anything.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Close-up sequence across one continuous architectural line.

**Images:** A carved spider in a pillar; a spider worked into a doorway; a raised spider relief above a temple; a house insignia displayed in metal.

**Narration:**
> Spiders did not walk the streets of Menzoberranzan, and no one expected the cavern floor to swarm with them.
>
> Their forms appeared instead in the city’s architecture.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Close-up on a drow face studying one of the carved spiders, absorbing the lesson.

**Image:** An adult male drow, framed tight, his eyes fixed on a stone carving just out of frame; respect and wariness in his stillness. Low warm light from one side, the rest in shadow.

**Narration:**
> They were warnings, symbols, and reminders.
>
> A drow learned early that there were some things it was wiser to observe than to touch, even when they were only stone.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Medium-wide shot at a city crossing.

**Image:** A male porter reaches a crossing at the same moment as a group of noble females. He stops, shifts his burden against his chest, and lowers his eyes. The females pass without slowing. When the bridge is clear, he continues on.

**Narration:**
> The city taught its laws through such small corrections.
>
> Its towers belonged to houses. Its bridges belonged to whoever could defend them. Its darkness belonged to anyone clever enough to use it.

### Panel 5 — Final beat

**Camera:** Near-black panel: a single lit doorway set into a towering wall of dark stone.

**Image:** Almost the entire panel is darkness. In one corner, a heavy carved doorway stands open, warm light spilling out onto the stone floor in a hard-edged quadrilateral — the only claimed light in the frame. Everything around it is unlit, unowned rock.

**Narration:**
> Everything else had to be claimed.

# Page 4 — No Separate Religion

> **Variety matrix:** P1 wide / temple entrance + offerings / dim sacred glow — P2 extreme-wide low angle / ritual hall from behind kneeling rows / cold altar light — P3 detail montage / pendant, lips, fingertips / intimate low light — P4 close-up / a child's face+hands copying the gesture / single warm candle.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Slow, reverent shot through a temple entrance.

**Image:** Spider carvings and symbols are integrated into black stone. Offerings sit in a shadowed sacred chamber.

**Narration:**
> There was no separate place in Menzoberranzan for religion and life.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Extreme-wide low angle from behind the kneeling rows of a ritual hall.

**Image:** The full hall: rows of kneeling drow seen from behind and slightly below, all facing a great carved image of the Spider Queen above a spider-emblazoned altar. A single priestess stands before it, arms raised. The architecture dwarfs the worshippers; cold light falls on the idol and nowhere else.

**Narration:**
> Lolth was not a subject taught during a particular hour before children returned to ordinary lessons.
>
> Her presence was woven through the city itself.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Close-up sequence of small human details.

**Images:** A spider pendant resting on a priestess's collarbone; careful lips forming a whispered prayer; fingertips releasing an offering into a dark opening in the stone.

**Narration:**
> The children of the noble houses learned the gestures before they understood the words.
>
> They watched offerings disappear into dark places and listened as prayers were spoken in voices that were careful not to sound uncertain.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Close-up on a child copying the adults’ gesture — face and small hands.

**Image:** A young drow child, tight framing on bowed head and small hands pressed together in an exact copy of the adult gesture. One warm candle lights the frame; the child's red eyes glance sideways, checking the others. No adult fully visible.

**Narration:**
> They repeated what they were told to repeat.
>
> They learned that Lolth watched.

# Page 5 — Above Them

> **Variety matrix:** P1 low wide / spider altar towering / cold idol light — P2 eye-level close group / bowed heads around a composed priestess / warm torchlight — P3 tall vertical composition / hierarchy totem, bottom-to-top / even formal light — P4 extreme wide / noble compound beneath temple heights / dusk ambience — P5 tight close-up / drow watching each other, not the idol / hard sliver light.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Low angle from beneath a vast spider-emblazoned altar.

**Image:** The altar dominates the frame. Lolth is not shown directly; her presence is conveyed through the symbol and the people kneeling beneath it.

**Narration:**
> She rewarded strength and cunning. She exposed weakness. She favored those who understood the struggle for power and destroyed those who mistook mercy for safety.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Eye-level, tight on a composed priestess framed by lowered heads.

**Image:** The priestess seen at eye level, calm and absolute, no raised voice needed. Around and before her, drow bow their heads into frame — their lowered postures are the sermon. Warm torchlight instead of idol-cold.

**Narration:**
> Her priestesses spoke with her authority.
>
> To disobey a priestess was not merely to disobey another drow. It was to place oneself against the goddess who stood behind her.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Vertical composition.

**Image:** From bottom to top: servants, noble houses, priestesses, and the carved spider symbol above them.

**Narration:**
> The lesson was simple because it needed to be simple.
>
> Lolth was above them.
>
> Her priestesses were above them.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Wide shot of a noble compound beneath the temple heights.

**Narration:**
> The noble houses stood beneath the authority of the priestesses, and within each house were further divisions of rank, privilege, blood, and usefulness.

### Panel 5 — Final beat

**Camera:** Close-up on several drow watching one another instead of looking upward.

**Narration:**
> Every drow occupied a place in that order.
>
> The drow did not need to be reminded that Lolth was watching.
>
> They reminded one another.

# Page 6 — Behavior Before Words

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Medium-wide interior shot.

**Image:** A noble female speaks while everyone else in the room waits in silence.

**Narration:**
> The city taught its lessons through behavior rather than explanation.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Low angle at a doorway.

**Image:** A priestess enters without saying a word. The posture of everyone inside the room changes around her.

**Narration:**
> A priestess could enter without saying a word and change the posture of everyone inside the room.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Tracking shot through a corridor.

**Image:** A servant carries a message past a warrior carrying a sword. Other drow glance toward the servant, measuring the importance of what he carries.

**Narration:**
> A servant could carry information farther than a warrior could carry a sword.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Side view of a young male stepping out of a noble female’s path.

**Image:** He possesses noble clothing and blood, but he moves aside before she reaches him.

**Narration:**
> A male could possess noble blood and still be expected to move aside when a woman approached.

### Panel 5 — Final beat

**Camera:** Close-up sequence of a command, a threat, and a silent person being allowed to speak.

**Narration:**
> Power did not always announce itself.
>
> Sometimes it was a command. Sometimes it was a threat. Sometimes it was simply a person being allowed to speak while everyone else remained silent.
>
> Children learned to watch for the difference.

# Page 7 — House Freth

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Wide exterior shot.

**Image:** House Freth is carved into a broad rise of dark stone. Sweeping arches open onto narrow balconies. Weapons hang where visitors can see them. House insignia and carved spider motifs recur across the structure.

**Narration:**
> House Freth was one of the great houses of Menzoberranzan.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Architectural montage moving from exterior to interior.

**Images:** A family chamber, a guarded gallery, a training room behind closed doors, shelves of records, and a fortified entrance.

**Narration:**
> A house was a family, but it was also a political faction, a military force, a store of knowledge, and a shield against every rival that might one day decide it was worth destroying.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Close-up on the House Freth insignia above an open doorway.

**Narration:**
> The name of a house could open a door.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Close-up on the same insignia above a sealed door in shadow.

**Narration:**
> It could also close one forever.

### Panel 5

**Camera:** Long interior corridor.

**Image:** Servants carry messages. Warriors train behind closed doors. Priestesses come and go. Noble daughters study customs and names. The house is busy but controlled.

**Narration:**
> The house was alive with movement.
>
> The house never truly slept.

# Page 8 — Waiting

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Medium-wide interior shot.

**Image:** A young boy sits in the shadow of a doorway, his feet not quite reaching the floor. He is small against the architecture and not yet named to the reader.

**Narration:**
> One of its rules was demonstrated in a long central hall.
>
> A boy sat in the shadow of a doorway, his feet not quite reaching the floor. He had been sent there to wait.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Low angle toward the middle of the hall.

**Image:** A male servant kneels before a House Freth priestess. The servant’s posture is rigid with fear.

**Narration:**
> A male servant knelt in the middle of the hall.
>
> The servant had made some mistake.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Close-up on the boy watching.

**Narration:**
> The boy did not know what it was.
>
> Perhaps the servant had carried a message to the wrong person. Perhaps he had spoken when silence was expected. Perhaps he had failed to anticipate what the priestess wanted from him.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Close-up on the priestess.

**Image:** Dark finery, composed expression, almost bored.

**Narration:**
> The reason hardly mattered.

### Panel 5 — Final beat

**Camera:** Wide shot holding all three figures in the hall.

**Image:** The boy remains in the doorway, the servant kneels, and the priestess stands before him without needing to raise her voice.

**Narration:**
> He had learned that being told to wait was not an invitation to ask why.

**Narration:**

**Narration:**

### Panel 4

**Narration:**

**Narration:**

**Narration:**

### Panel 5 — Final beat

**Camera:** Close-up on Layn lowering his eyes as a priestess passes.

**Narration:**

# Page 13 — Ordinary Things

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Tracking shot following Layn through House Freth.

**Image:** Smooth arches disappear into darkness. Stairways lead to higher galleries. Weapons hang along walls. Carved spiders and webs repeat through the architecture. The insignia appears everywhere.

**Narration:**
> The architecture of House Freth unfolded around him as he walked.
>
> The things that might have seemed frightening or unnatural to an outsider became ordinary when they surrounded you from birth.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Close-up montage.

**Images:** Darkness; displayed weapons; carved spider reliefs; whispered conversations behind a half-open door; a servant waiting for permission; a noble female passing without acknowledgment.

**Narration:**
> The darkness, the weapons, the carved spiders, the whispered conversations, and the constant calculation of who could command whom were all ordinary.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Wide interior composition.

**Image:** House Freth reads as both home and fortress. Children learn, servants work, warriors train behind closed doors, and the house’s defenses remain visible.

**Narration:**
> The house taught its children that a home could be a fortress and a fortress could be a family. It taught them that loyalty was valuable because it was useful, that obedience protected those who knew when to offer it, and that affection had little place in decisions that concerned the survival of the house.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** City-level montage beyond the house.

**Images:** A merchant argues over a shipment; a priestess delivers a command; noble houses exchange polite words; a servant carries a message.

**Narration:**
> Somewhere beyond the halls, the city continued its endless life.

### Panel 5 — Final beat

**Camera:** Split composition.

**Images:** One child is taught to pray before a carved spider symbol; somewhere else, another child learns how to kill.

**Narration:**
> Somewhere, a child was being taught to pray to Lolth.
>
> Somewhere else, another child was learning how to kill.

# Page 14 — Nothing Remarkable

**Purpose:** Final emotional beat. The page should feel quiet and cinematic. Layn is established as insignificant within House Freth and beneath the attention of the goddess.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Extreme wide shot pulling away from House Freth into the city.

**Image:** The city’s ancient architecture dwarfs the house and the boy within it. Names and structures vanish into the darkness beyond.

**Narration:**
> Menzoberranzan had existed long before the boy was born, with its own rhythms, grudges, traditions, and memory; houses rose and fell, names disappeared, and new names took their places.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Wide shot of the city continuing without pause.

**Narration:**
> The city did not care.
>
> The city endured.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Medium shot of young Layn alone in a House Freth corridor.

**Narration:**
> For now, the boy was only one more child within House Freth.
>
> A male among thousands.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** High angle, Layn reduced to a small figure among larger architectural forms.

**Narration:**
> A small piece in a city built from larger pieces.
>
> There was nothing remarkable about him.
>
> Nothing that demanded the attention of a goddess.

### Panel 5 — Final beat

**Layout:** Black or almost entirely black panel. Layn is barely visible; only his red eyes catch a faint glimmer.

**Narration:**
> His name was Laynlindr Freth.
>
> Layn.

**Purpose:** End on the exact thematic beat: Layn is currently beneath notice. Do not foreshadow his psionic powers, later spider trauma, or the academy in this chapter.

# Production Notes

- **Target length:** 14 pages for the complete adaptation.
- The comic adapts only the finalized prose of I.1 — Beneath the Spider’s City.
- The story order is deliberate: city and architecture, drow religion and hierarchy, House Freth, then the servant punishment and Layn’s final reveal.
- Spider imagery in the city and House Freth should be carved, worked into architecture, or displayed as insignia. Do not depict spiders walking through city streets or swarming across ordinary floors.
- The carved spider in the servant scene is the visual trigger for the servant’s fatal glance; it is not a living creature.
- The execution should be disturbing and consequential, but the horror should come primarily from the priestess’s calm authority and the household’s immediate return to routine.
- The human-slave detail should appear as a later fact Layn learns, not as a reason for the servant’s punishment.
- Layn should be portrayed as a quiet, observant young noble: above servants by blood, below older noble males by age, and far below the women and priestesses who control the house.
- Do not depict Layn interacting with the Matron Mother in this chapter. She appears only as the summit of the hierarchy.
- Do not introduce the family’s conspiracy, the bed scene, psionic suspicion, academy training, or later spider trauma here; those belong to I.2 and I.3.
- Do not depict Lolth as personally interested in Layn yet. The final page establishes the opposite.
- The prose section remains the narrative source, while this document translates it into sequential visual storytelling.
- Do not create final art directly from this document until the panel script and visual continuity have been reviewed.
- Future image-generation prompts should inherit the global style from characters/laynlindr-freth/backstory/chapter-1/comic/style.md and should be stored separately from this script.
