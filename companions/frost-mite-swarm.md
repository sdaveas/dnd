# Swarm of Frost Mites

**Medium Beast (Swarm), unaligned**

This creature is summoned by Layn's **Frosted Piper's Flute**.

## Core statistics

| Stat | Value |
|---|---:|
| AC | 12 (natural armor) |
| HP | 12 (3d6 + 3) |
| Speed | 20 ft.; climb 20 ft.; burrow 5 ft. (ice and snow only) |
| STR | 3 (-4) |
| DEX | 13 (+1) |
| CON | 10 (+0) |
| INT | 1 (-5) |
| WIS | 7 (-2) |
| CHA | 1 (-5) |
| Challenge | 1/2 (100 XP) |

## Resistances / immunities / vulnerabilities

- Damage vulnerability: **Fire**
- Damage immunity: **Cold**
- Condition immunities: **Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned**

## Senses

- Blindsight 10 ft.
- Tremorsense 20 ft.
- Passive Perception 8

## Swarm

The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny insect.

The swarm can't regain hit points or gain temporary hit points.

## Ravenous Cold

The frost mites devour organic material for warmth. At the end of each of the swarm's turns, nonmagical objects made of wood, cloth, leather, or rope in the swarm's space take **5 (2d4) cold damage**.

## False Stillness

While the swarm remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a patch of frost or snow.

## Actions

### Bites
**Melee Weapon Attack:** +3 to hit, reach 0 ft., one target in the swarm's space.

**Hit:** 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) cold damage, or 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) cold damage if the swarm has half its hit points or fewer.

On a hit, the target's speed is reduced by **10 feet until the start of the swarm's next turn**.

## Flute relationship

The swarm's behavior depends on whether it is a **Friendly swarm** under the Frosted Piper's Flute:

- A Friendly swarm obeys Layn's commands.
- If Layn gives it no commands, it defends itself but otherwise takes no actions.
- If a Friendly swarm starts its turn more than 30 ft from Layn, Layn's control ends.
- After control ends this way, the swarm behaves normally and cannot be swayed by the flute's music for 24 hours.

The flute itself can summon one swarm per use, with the property refreshing at the next dawn.

## Simulator notes

Track the swarm as a separate combatant with its own HP, position, movement, immunities, vulnerabilities, condition immunities, and action state.

Because its INT is **1 (-5)**, its autonomous behavior should be modeled accordingly when it is not Friendly/controlled rather than treating it as an intelligent tactical creature.
