# Comic Script — I.1: Beneath the Spider's City

> **Status:** Revised comic adaptation draft.
>
> This document defines the sequential-art content of I.1: pages, panels, composition, narration, dialogue, sound effects, and section-specific continuity. Global visual direction lives in `backstory/story/comic/style.md`.

---

# Page 1 — No Sky

### Panel 1 — Full-page splash

**Camera:** Extreme wide shot across the cavern.

**Image:** Menzoberranzan fills a gigantic subterranean cavern. Towers rise from the floor and climb immense rock formations. Stalactites disappear into darkness above. Bridges span huge gaps. Tiny magical lights reveal fragments of the city. Individual drow are almost impossible to distinguish.

**Narration:**
> There was no sky above Menzoberranzan.
>
> There had never been one.

**Purpose:** Establish scale, darkness, and the city as a character in its own right.

# Page 2 — Home

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Wide architectural shot.

**Image:** Enormous stone columns, carved towers, bridges and distant houses.

**Narration:**

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Close-up on drow moving naturally through the darkness.

**Narration:**

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Upward angle toward enormous stalactites.

**Narration:**
> The darkness was ordinary.

# Page 4 — Punishment

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Dramatic close-up on the priestess raising one hand.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Wide shot.

**Image:** Violet magical energy passes through the servant's body.

**SFX:** *VMMMM*

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Close-up / unsettling transformation.

**Image:** His face remains still. He does not react.

**Narration:**

### Panel 5

### Panel 6

**Narration:**

# Page 14 — Nothing Worth Noticing

**Purpose:** Final emotional beat. The page should feel quiet and cinematic. The reader should leave with Layn established as insignificant to Lolth, while the final line creates the dramatic irony that this will not remain true.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Wide shot of young Layn standing alone in a House Freth corridor.

**Narration:**
> For now, the boy was only one more child within House Freth.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Medium shot.

**Image:** Layn surrounded by the enormous architecture of the house.

**Narration:**
> A male among thousands.
>
> A small piece in a city built from larger pieces.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Extreme wide shot pulling away from Layn, House Freth, and finally the city.

**Narration:**
> There was nothing remarkable about him.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Extreme close-up / near-black composition. Layn is barely visible in darkness, with only his red eyes catching a faint glimmer.

**Narration:**
> His name was Laynlindr Freth.

### Panel 5 — Final beat

**Layout:** Black or almost entirely black panel.

**Narration:**
> And as far as the Spider Queen was concerned—
>
> **nothing worth noticing.**

**Purpose:** End on the exact thematic beat: Layn is currently beneath Lolth's notice. The reader knows this will change.

# Production Notes

- **Target length:** 14 pages for the current complete adaptation.
- The comic should adapt the revised Chapter 1 rather than the earlier draft.
- The servant punishment is the first major visual demonstration of Menzoberranzan's rules.
- Layn should be portrayed as observant and quiet, not as a child who already understands the full implications of drow society.
- His mother should never be depicted interacting with him. Her defining action toward him is indifference.
- Do not introduce invented friends, companions, or escape partners.
- Do not depict Layn's later spider trauma here; that belongs to I.3.
- Do not depict Lolth as personally interested in Layn yet. The final panel establishes the opposite.
- The prose section remains separate and defines the underlying narrative. This script translates that narrative into sequential visual storytelling.
- Do not create final art directly from this document until the panel script and visual continuity have been reviewed.
- Future image-generation prompts should inherit the global style from `backstory/story/comic/style.md` and should be stored separately from this script.
