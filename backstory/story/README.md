# Layn — Story Draft

This directory contains the prose version of Layn's backstory, developed separately from `backstory/notes.md`.

`backstory/notes.md` remains the source of truth for story decisions, canon constraints, unresolved details, and planning.

The story is organized by **Part → Section → Subsection**, with each subsection intended to represent a scene or tightly connected sequence. The outline below is the structural plan for the complete backstory. Individual titles and scene boundaries may evolve as the prose is written.

## Narrative Approach

The story combines two lenses:

- **Layn's perspective:** an intimate view of his life and development as a young drow. His understanding is limited by his age and circumstances; he should not think with the knowledge of an outside observer.
- **Omniscient perspective:** a wider view of Menzoberranzan, drow society, politics, religion, customs, and history, including information Layn does not yet understand.

Worldbuilding should be embedded in lived scenes wherever possible rather than presented as encyclopedia-style exposition.

The prose should have its own voice. *Homeland* is a reference for immersive worldbuilding and narrative architecture, not a template for copying R. A. Salvatore's prose style.

The final prose should read naturally aloud: varied sentence length, clear paragraph rhythm, strong imagery, dialogue that is easy to follow when spoken, and enough breathing room for atmosphere and emotional beats.
