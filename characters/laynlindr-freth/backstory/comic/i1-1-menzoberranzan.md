# Page 1 — No Sky

### Panel 1 — Full-page splash

**Camera:** Extreme wide shot across the cavern.

**Image:** Menzoberranzan fills a gigantic, arrowhead-shaped subterranean cavern. Towers rise from the floor and climb immense calcite formations. Stalactites disappear into darkness above. Bridges span huge gaps, joining towers carved directly from living rock. Narbondel, the city's great central pillar, stands in the distance with a faint band of orange light around it. Tiny magical lights reveal fragments of the city; individual drow are almost impossible to distinguish.

**Narration:**
> There was no sky above Menzoberranzan.
>
> There had never been one.

**Purpose:** Establish scale, darkness, and the city as a character in its own right. No spiders walking the city.

**Reuse:** existing `pages/page-01/panel-01.png` matches.

# Page 2 — The Inverted World

> **Variety matrix:** P1 wide low-angle / columns + stalactites / cool ambient — P2 long perspective / bridges between towers / faerie-fire edge light — P3 high angle looking down / clinging districts / mixed glow — P4 medium-wide / Narbondel with heat band / warm-vs-cold contrast.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Wide architectural shot looking up through the cavern.

**Image:** Great columns climb from the depths toward an unseen ceiling. Enormous stalactites hang above like the teeth of a sleeping beast. High on the cavern roof, distant paths and houses cling to the stone above the city.

**Narration:**
> There was only stone, rising so far above the city that even the sharpest eyes could not find where the cavern ended and darkness began.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Long perspective across bridges and towers.

**Image:** Bridges and walkways cross the emptiness between columns, joining towers carved from the living rock.

**Narration:**
> Bridges and walkways crossed the emptiness between them.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** High angle looking down the cavern walls.

**Image:** Houses cling to the cavern walls; others rise from the floor in defensive spires. Paths and galleries cross the roof high overhead — from below they seem to hang in the air. An inverted world above the city.

**Narration:**
> Some houses clung to the cavern walls. Others rose from the floor in defensive spires.
>
> From below, the highest districts hung above the city like a second, inverted world.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Medium-wide on Narbondel, the great central pillar.

**Image:** At the cavern's center stands Narbondel — a huge natural pillar serving as the city's clock. A band of orange warmth climbs its stone, read from every street, bridge, and balcony by drow eyes that see warmth the way surface folk see color.

**Narration:**
> At the city's heart stood Narbondel — a pillar of stone that served as its clock.
>
> When the band of warmth reached its end and faded, Menzoberranzan entered its long night.

# Page 3 — Light Gives Edges

> **Variety matrix:** P1 wide vista / faerie-fire colors / cold multi-source glow — P2 macro / fungi on stone / own dim glow — P3 darkness study / unlit doorway and balcony / near-black — P4 street level / drow at ease in the dark / warm street glow.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Wide vista across towers and gates.

**Image:** Blue, green, and violet faerie fire outlines towers, gates, and house compounds in cold radiance — the city's architecture drawn in lines of light.

**Narration:**
> Blue, green, and violet faerie fire burned across the city, outlining towers, gates, and house compounds in cold radiance.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Macro close-up on a cavern wall.

**Image:** Pale fungi cling to the deeper walls, adding their own weak glow against black stone.

**Narration:**
> Pale fungi clung to the deeper walls and added their own weak glow.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Darkness study — an unlit doorway, an empty balcony, a stretch of roof.

**Image:** Between the lights: a doorway swallowed whole, a balcony with nothing beyond it, a long unlit stretch of roof. The dark has texture here — weight, presence.

**Narration:**
> None of the light defeated the darkness. It only gave the darkness edges — leaving doorways and entire stretches of roof to whatever waited beyond attention.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Street-level view of drow moving naturally through the darkness.

**Image:** Merchants, servants, warriors, priestesses, and children move beneath the lights at ease. No live spiders cross the streets; spider imagery appears only in distant carved architecture.

**Narration:**
> To someone from the surface, Menzoberranzan might have seemed to exist in perpetual night.
>
> To the drow, it was simply home.

# Page 4 — The City Is Not Silent

> **Variety matrix:** P1 wide market-street / merchants in shadowed stalls / warm stall glow — P2 medium-wide processional / crowd parting / high-contrast key light — P3 silhouette study / a figure vanishing into an alley mouth / swallowed by dark — P4 medium / drow at ease at home / intimate low light.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Wide street scene in a market district.

**Image:** Footsteps cross the bridges overhead. Merchants call from shadowed stalls. The city's noise made visible — commerce in the dark.

**Narration:**
> The city was not silent.
>
> Merchants called from shadowed stalls, and footsteps crossed the bridges overhead.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Medium-wide along a procession route.

**Image:** Servants carry messages beneath the eyes of armed guards. A noble procession moves through the traffic — and the crowd opens before it without needing to be told.

**Narration:**
> Servants carried messages beneath the eyes of armed guards.
>
> Noble processions moved through the traffic, and the crowd opened before them without needing to be told.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Close on an alley mouth; a lone figure stepping in.

**Image:** A silhouette walks into an unlit gap between two towers and is swallowed. No scream, no trace — just absence.

**Narration:**
> Darkness concealed. It protected.
>
> It gave the unwary somewhere to disappear.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Medium shot, drow at ease in a doorway or stair.

**Image:** A drow standing relaxed in deep shadow, at home in it — the way surface folk stand in sunlight.

**Narration:**
> It was as natural to the drow as the stone beneath their feet —
>
> and just as unforgiving when someone misjudged its weight.

# Page 5 — What They Were

> **Purpose:** The origin flashback. Visual language inverts: warm light, open space, surface world — sinking page by panel into the dark. The only page of the issue that shows the sun.

> **Variety matrix:** P1 wide bright / sunlit forest elves / warm daylight — P2 long descending / tunnels into the deep / light dying behind — P3 generations montage / stone remaking them / mixed eras — P4 extreme close-up / dark eyes with no memory / single cold light.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Wide, bright — the only bright page-opening of the issue.

**Image:** Memory of the surface: elves in a sunlit forest, gold and green, open sky above. It should feel like another world — because it is.

**Narration:**
> They were not always what they had become.
>
> The drow had been elves of the surface once — children of the sun and the open sky.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Long descending composition, following figures into the earth.

**Image:** A corruption takes them; they follow a dark power down long tunnels into the deep earth. The light dies panel-left to right — the last glimmer of day at their backs.

**Narration:**
> Then a corruption took them, and drove them out of the light.
>
> They followed a dark power down long tunnels into the deep earth.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Montage across generations — the same bloodline changing.

**Image:** Generation by generation, the deep earth remakes them: skin darkening, eyes reddening, features sharpening for the dark. Stone halls replace forest shelters.

**Narration:**
> Generation by generation, the deep earth remade them —
>
> until the sun they had been born under became an enemy, and the stone beneath them a home.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Extreme close-up on adult drow eyes.

**Image:** Modern drow eyes — dark, beautiful, empty of the sun. What was lost is not mourned because it is not remembered.

**Narration:**
> They did not remember the change.
>
> They remembered what they were taught.

# Page 6 — Traitor

> **Variety matrix:** P1 intimate close / elder teaching a child the word / warm firelight — P2 detail study / enemy mural or tapestry / formal light — P3 wide / warriors at a tunnel mouth glaring up / cold depth light.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Close, intimate — an elder's mouth beside a child's ear.

**Image:** A drow elder teaches a child to speak; the lesson and the hatred are the same lesson. The child's mouth forms the word.

**Narration:**
> The hatred came before language.
>
> They learned to curse the name of their surface kin from the same mouths that taught them to speak.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Detail study of a mural or woven tapestry in a noble hall.

**Image:** Surface elves depicted as they are taught — betrayers, enemies, rendered in hostile stylized form. The drow word for them carved beneath: traitor. (No readable text in the image; the carving implies it.)

**Narration:**
> The word their tongue made for those elves meant traitor.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Wide — warriors at a great tunnel mouth ascending toward the surface, faces turned up toward a darkness that leads to light they hate.

**Image:** Armed drow glaring up a long ascending tunnel, postures hard, the road to the surface world a pale threat far above.

**Narration:**
> Of all their enemies, the drow counted the surface elves the deadliest.
>
> The single hatred they would keep alive across whole centuries of stone.

# Page 7 — Born to It

> **Variety matrix:** P1 close, intimate / child conjuring dancing motes / warm-violet sparkle in dark — P2 triptych / mote, darkness-shroud, cold-fire target / three light states — P3 medium / drow seeing in the dark / eyes catching faint light — P4 silhouette with blade / near-black / one edge light.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Close on a drow child barely able to stand, calling light.

**Image:** A toddler-age drow girl summons dancing motes above her palms — her first spell, played with like a toy. Violet light plays on her face.

**Narration:**
> A drow child who could barely stand already called light to her fingers.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Triptych of three spells — one wide image.

**Scene:** (1) dancing motes teasing the unwary; (2) a shroud of darkness swallowing a whole room; (3) cold fire outlining a target like a brand.

**Narration:**
> Dancing motes to tease the unwary. A shroud of darkness to swallow a room. Cold fire to paint a target so none could slip away.
>
> A birthright older than the Underdark — turned below into a set of weapons.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Medium — drow eyes in full darkness, seeing what is there.

**Image:** A drow standing in a black corridor we cannot see into — but she can. Her posture reads the space like text.

**Narration:**
> The dark was never empty to them. They saw further into it than any surface eye, and read it like a language.

### Panel 4

**Camera:** Silhouette study — a drow with a blade in near-total darkness.

**Image:** Almost nothing visible: one figure, one blade edge catching a thread of light.

**Narration:**
> It was where they lived, where they had buried themselves —
>
> and where they had learned how to kill.

# Page 8 — Everything Else Had to Be Claimed

> **Variety matrix:** P1 detail montage / carved spider studies / formal frontal light — P2 close / hand near carving, not touching / hard rim light — P3 wide / guarded bridge between house territories / cold key — P4 near-black final beat / one claimed light / single warm source.

### Panel 1

**Camera:** Detail montage along one architectural line.

**Images:** A spider carved into a pillar; one worked around a doorway arch; one raised above a temple; a house insignia in dark metal.

**Narration:**
> Spiders filled the city's architecture — carved into pillars, worked into doorways, raised above temples, woven into the emblems of houses.

### Panel 2

**Camera:** Close-up on a drow hand passing near a carved spider — not touching.

**Narration:**
> They were warnings, symbols, and reminders.
>
> A drow learned early that some things it was wiser to observe than to touch — even when they were only stone.

### Panel 3

**Camera:** Wide — a guarded bridge crossing between two house territories.

**Image:** Watchers at a gatehouse; traffic passing under the eyes of whoever holds the bridge.

**Narration:**
> Its towers belonged to houses. Its bridges carried traffic under the watch of whoever controlled the territory.

### Panel 4 — Final beat

**Camera:** Near-black panel: a single lit doorway set into a towering wall of dark stone.

**Narration:**
> Its darkness belonged to anyone clever enough to use it.
>
> Everything else had to be claimed.

**Purpose:** The page and section end on the claiming beat — the thesis of Menzoberranzan, and the standard the boy's city will hold him to.
