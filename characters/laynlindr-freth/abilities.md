# Layn — Abilities & Features

## Rogue features

### Expertise
Double proficiency bonus on selected skills. Current expertise is recorded in `skills.md`.

### Sneak Attack
Once per turn, deal extra **4d6** damage to one creature hit with a Finesse or Ranged weapon when the attack qualifies for Sneak Attack.

The player chooses which qualifying attack receives Sneak Attack when multiple attacks hit.

### Thieves' Cant
Layn knows Thieves' Cant and one other language of choice.

### Weapon Mastery
Current mastery: **Vex** on Psychic Blades. Mastery choices can change after a Long Rest.

### Cunning Action
Bonus Action: Dash, Disengage, or Hide.

### Steady Aim
Bonus Action: if Layn has not moved during the turn, gain advantage on the next attack roll this turn. After using it, Speed becomes 0 for the turn.

### Cunning Strike
When dealing Sneak Attack damage, Layn may forgo Sneak Attack dice to apply an additional effect.

| Option | Cost | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Poison | 1d6 | CON save or Poisoned for 1 minute; requires Poisoner's Kit |
| Trip | 1d6 | DEX save or Prone; target must be Large or smaller |
| Withdraw | 1d6 | Move up to half Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks |
| Daze | 2d6 | Not available at level 7 |
| Obscure | 3d6 | Not available at level 7 |
| Knock Out | 6d6 | Not available at level 7 |

Only options available at Layn's current level may be used.

### Uncanny Dodge
Reaction: when an attacker Layn can see hits with an attack roll, halve that attack's damage, rounding down.

### Evasion
When subjected to an effect allowing a DEX save for half damage:

- Successful save: **0 damage**.
- Failed save: **half damage**.

Cannot use Evasion while Incapacitated.

### Reliable Talent
For an ability check using a skill or tool proficiency, a d20 result of 9 or lower counts as **10**.

**Important:** Reliable Talent applies to ability checks only. It does not apply to attack rolls or saving throws.

## Soulknife

### Psionic Energy Dice
Current pool: **6d8** at level 7.

- Regain 1 die on a Short Rest.
- Regain all spent dice on a Long Rest.

### Psi-Bolstered Knack
When Layn fails an ability check using a proficient skill or tool, Layn may roll one Psionic Energy Die and add the result. The die is expended only if the check then succeeds.

### Psychic Whispers
Magic Action: choose up to Proficiency Bonus creatures Layn can see and roll one Psionic Energy Die. For a number of hours equal to the roll, the chosen creatures can communicate telepathically with Layn within 1 mile.

The first use after a Long Rest is free.

### Psychic Blades
Whenever Layn takes the Attack action or makes an Opportunity Attack, Layn can manifest a Psychic Blade in a free hand.

**Main blade:**
- 1d6 Psychic + DEX modifier
- Finesse
- Thrown 60/120 ft
- Vex mastery

**Bonus blade:**
- After attacking with the main blade, Layn can make a second attack as a Bonus Action if the other hand is free.
- 1d4 Psychic + DEX modifier

A Psychic Blade vanishes after it hits or misses and leaves no physical mark.

## Drow traits

- **Darkvision:** 120 ft
- **Dancing Lights:** Cantrip; spellcasting ability chosen at character creation
- **Faerie Fire:** 1/day; always prepared; can also be cast with spell slots
- **Darkness:** 1/day; always prepared; can also be cast with spell slots
- **Fey Ancestry:** Advantage on saves to avoid or end Charmed; magic cannot put Layn to sleep
- **Keen Senses:** Proficiency in one of Insight, Perception, or Survival as chosen at creation
- **Trance:** No sleep required; Long Rest completes in 4 hours of trancelike meditation while retaining consciousness

## Not yet available

These Soulknife features are future-level abilities and must not be used at level 7:

- Level 9: Soul Blades — Homing Strikes, Psychic Teleportation
- Level 13: Psychic Veil
- Level 17: Rend Mind
