# Layn's Story — Character Cheatsheet

This is the continuity sheet for characters in Layn's backstory and its adaptations. It is separate from [`character.md`](../character.md), which contains Layn's D&D mechanics.

## Status labels

- **CANON** — established by Forgotten Realms source material.
- **OUR STORY** — an addition created for Layn's personal narrative.
- **UNKNOWN** — intentionally unresolved; do not fill it in casually.

## Canon rule

Use the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/) as the default research index, then follow its cited primary sources for consequential claims. Keep canon, our additions, and unresolved details visibly separate.

House Freth reference: [Forgotten Realms Wiki — House Freth](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/House_Freth).

## Characters

### Laynlindr Freth — “Layn”

- **Status:** OUR STORY character placed within the canon history of House Freth.
- **Role:** Young male noble and the youngest member of House Freth; later a drow rogue and Soulknife.
- **Timeline:** born about 1287 DR; the Hearing and Melee-Magthere at 20 (1307 DR); graduates 1317 DR; flees the 1319 DR attack at about 32 and is declared dead. See [`notes.md` §24](notes.md#24-part-one-revision-decisions).
- **Address:** The narrator calls him Layn. Family and household figures generally use “boy”, “male”, or “youngling”, not his name.
- **Description:** Quiet, observant, physically slight in childhood, and trained to make himself unobtrusive. His early fear and vigilance later become the foundation of his stealth.
- **Core conflict:** He is shaped by drow society while gradually recognizing that its values are not his own.
- **Hidden potential:** Psionic abilities manifest early, instinctively, and without his understanding. His sisters try to suppress them through fear.
- **Relationships:** Neglected by his mother until the Hearing; tormented and conditioned by his sisters with spiders from age 10 until Zilvrae ends the routine years before the Hearing; underestimated by the household; trained by Mistress Zilvrae; sent to Melee-Magthere after the Hearing.
- **Lasting marks:** a deep fear of spiders, hypervigilance around rooms and bedding, and the ability to stay still under extreme stress.
- **Voice:** The narrator may be eloquent and wide-ranging, but Layn's childhood understanding must remain limited by age and experience.
- **Arc:** Insignificant young male → frightened and hypervigilant child → trained soldier → stealth-focused rogue with uncontrolled psionic potential.

### Dreydre Freth

- **Status:** CANON name; OUR STORY relationship, age, motives, and childhood history.
- **Role:** Elder sister of Layn and a noble woman of House Freth.
- **Age in our story:** About 130 at Layn's birth and about 140 in Chapter 4; the source material does not establish her age. She finished Arach-Tinilith before Layn was born and returns for advanced study during Part Two.
- **Fate:** survives the 1319 DR attack as the canon survivor who "had just finished training at Arach-Tinilith"; taken into House Baenre with Greyanna and an unnamed male.
- **Description:** Controlled, strategic, and socially dangerous. She treats cruelty as a tool and avoids displaying uncertainty in front of anyone who might use it against her.
- **Motives:** Preserve her status, prevent Layn from outshining the females of the house, and keep a potentially dangerous male power from reaching the Matron Mother or a magical academy.
- **Relationship to Layn:** She helps turn the spider incidents into deliberate psychological conditioning.
- **Relationship to Greyanna:** Senior collaborator and intellectual driver of the conspiracy; their alliance contains rivalry beneath its practical unity.
- **Voice:** Restrained, precise, contemptuous without theatrical explanation. She prefers implication, tests, and commands.
- **Canon note:** The House Freth reference places Dreydre in House Freth operations circa 1369 DR but does not establish a direct relationship to Layn.

### Greyanna Freth

- **Status:** CANON name; OUR STORY relationship, age, motives, and childhood history.
- **Role:** Elder sister of Layn and Dreydre's collaborator.
- **Age in our story:** About 75 at Layn's birth and about 85 in Chapter 4; the source material does not establish her age. She finished Arach-Tinilith before Layn was born.
- **Fate:** survives the 1319 DR attack; taken into House Baenre with Dreydre and an unnamed male.
- **Description:** Younger than Dreydre but already shaped by House Freth's expectations. More reactive and visibly curious than Dreydre, though not softer toward Layn.
- **Motives:** Protect her standing, avoid being eclipsed by a male sibling, and help contain the danger represented by Layn's possible psionics.
- **Relationship to Layn:** Participates in the spider conditioning and the plan to steer him toward the warriors' academy.
- **Relationship to Dreydre:** Follows Dreydre's lead but contributes her own observations, doubts about facts, and tactical suggestions; she does not express concern for Layn's comfort.
- **Voice:** Quicker and more probing than Dreydre's. She asks questions to test risk, not to offer mercy.
- **Canon note:** The House Freth reference places Greyanna in House Freth operations circa 1369 DR but does not establish a direct relationship to Layn.

### Matron Mother of House Freth

- **Status:** CANON role; OUR STORY personal relationship and unnamed identity.
- **Name:** UNKNOWN. Use the title rather than inventing a personal name until the story requires one.
- **Role:** Supreme authority within House Freth and the highest point in Layn's childhood hierarchy.
- **Description:** Powerful, distant, and difficult to read. Her attention is dangerous; her indifference is its own form of punishment. She wears robes the dark red of dried blood and a circlet of eight black metal legs meeting at the brow over two facing spiders. Her spirit at the Hearing wears the same circlet and robe.
- **Relationship to Layn:** She ignores him entirely until the Hearing, where she meets his eyes and speaks to him for the first time ("Show me."). She has known of his possible psionics since his infancy (Chapter 10). Her order: watch him from a distance, do not provoke or hurt him merely for evidence, and suppress any manifestation. Her reason for "Keep him alive." is **UNKNOWN**.
- **Relationship to the sisters:** Her rage is a risk the sisters fear, but ordinary cruelty toward a male is not suspicious to her.
- **Voice:** Rare, economical, and unquestionable. Silence and dismissal should do most of her work.

### Mistress Zilvrae

- **Status:** OUR STORY character.
- **Role:** Female in-house instructor. Born a daughter of a lesser house destroyed by another house (not Freth). She survived as a noble witness, so the attackers were destroyed, and what remained of her was given to House Freth. Her rank is real; her house is gone, and she never names it. (Foreshadow: House Freth will fall the same way, and its survivors will be given to House Baenre.)
- **Description:** Competent, formal, and focused on standards rather than affection. Helpful, wise, and knowledgeable, but emotionally distant and cold, like a Jarvis or an Alfred. Otherwise true neutral, almost an automaton. "Cunning disengagement" (Chapter 7) is the closest she comes to a joke.
- **Hidden beliefs (never stated openly):** an anarchist in a city with no word for it. She believes males and females are equal, and that each child should follow the path it is good at rather than be used as a house asset. Her Chapter 6 lesson delivers these beliefs disguised as house law.
- **Powers:** no psionic gift. Her manifested weapons are magic; Layn's later blades come from his mind.
- **Fate:** dies in the 1319 DR attack, sacrificing herself to save Layn (see `notes.md` §6).
- **Relationship to Layn:** Has known of his possible psionics since his infancy and obeys the Matron Mother's order not to lead him inward. Within that order she argues for his development, ends the spider routine (Chapter 8), and teaches what she believes under cover of house law. She never offers him emotional refuge.
- **Voice:** Instructional, exact, and status-conscious. Praise is rare and correction is public when it serves the house.

## Continuity rules for new characters

- Search the Forgotten Realms Wiki before inventing a named canon-adjacent character.
- Do not assign ages, blood relationships, survival outcomes, or chronology to canon characters without marking the addition **OUR STORY**.
- Give every recurring character a distinct pressure, motive, and way of speaking.
- Add a character here before giving them a major speaking role in the novel, audio, or comic adaptation.
- If a character is intentionally unnamed, keep the title or role consistent across every section and adaptation.
