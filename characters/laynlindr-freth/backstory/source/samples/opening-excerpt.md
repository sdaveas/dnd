Part I — The Child of House Freth

Chapter One — Beneath the Spider's City

There was no sky above Menzoberranzan.

There had never been one.

There was only stone, rising so far above the city that even the sharpest eyes could not find where the cavern ended and the darkness began. Great columns of rock climbed from the depths toward the unseen ceiling, while enormous stalactites hung above them like the teeth of some sleeping beast. Bridges and walkways crossed the empty spaces between them, connecting towers that had been carved directly from the living stone.

In places, magical lights burned with cold violet or blue radiance. Elsewhere, pale fungi clung to the rock and gave off their own dim glow. The illumination was never enough to banish the darkness. It merely shaped it, leaving vast regions of the cavern untouched.

To someone from the surface, Menzoberranzan might have seemed to exist in perpetual night.
